Privacy Policy

AI Content Factory

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Overview

AI Content Factory is a personal workflow used to create, organize, and publish educational video content. This policy explains how information is handled when the workflow connects to Google services.

Information accessed

Depending on the workflow configuration and permissions granted, the application may access Google Sheets data used to manage content and YouTube account functions needed to upload videos.

How information is used

Information is used to process content workflow tasks, prepare publishing details, and upload videos to the authorized YouTube channel. It is not sold or used for advertising.

Storage and sharing

Content and workflow data may be stored in the Google services and local files configured by the owner. Information is not intentionally shared with unrelated third parties, except as needed to operate the connected services or comply with law.

Data security

Reasonable steps are taken to protect credentials and workflow files. Access to connected Google services depends on the permissions granted by the account owner.

Data retention and deletion

Workflow data may be retained in spreadsheets, local files, or connected services until the owner deletes it. Google account access can be revoked through the Google Account security settings.
